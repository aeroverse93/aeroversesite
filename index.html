01 / AIRCRAFT

Know your aircraft.

Every aircraft is a carefully designed combination of structures, systems and aerodynamic surfaces.

01

Fuselage

The main body that houses passengers, crew, equipment and cargo.

02

Wings

Generate lift and may contain fuel tanks, control surfaces and other systems.

03

Empennage

The tail assembly provides stability and directional control.

04

Landing Gear

Supports the aircraft on the ground and absorbs landing loads.

02 / AIRCRAFT ENGINES

Power behind flight.

Aircraft engines convert energy into useful thrust. The two major families you'll encounter are piston engines and gas turbines.

01

Piston Engine

Common in many training and light aircraft. A piston engine burns fuel and air inside cylinders to drive a crankshaft and propeller.

  • Cylinders
  • Pistons
  • Crankshaft
  • Propeller
02

Gas Turbine

Used widely in modern aviation. Air is compressed, mixed with fuel and burned, then expanded through turbine stages to create power and thrust.

  • Compressor
  • Combustion chamber
  • Turbine
  • Exhaust

03 / AVIATION HISTORY

A century of flight.

1783

First successful hot-air balloon

The Montgolfier brothers demonstrated manned balloon flight in France.

1903

Wright brothers

Orville and Wilbur Wright achieved controlled, powered flight at Kitty Hawk.

1914–1918

World War I aviation

Aircraft developed rapidly for reconnaissance, combat and pilot training.

1939–1945

World War II

Major advances in speed, engines, aerodynamics, weapons and aircraft production.

1950s+

The Jet Age

Jet propulsion transformed commercial and military aviation.

Today

Modern aviation

Advanced materials, avionics, automation and efficient engines continue to reshape flight.
